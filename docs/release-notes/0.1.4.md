Buttons light under the pointer, the release notes' scrollbar can be dragged, and an optional Mica or Acrylic window backdrop.

- Every button lights up under the pointer and darkens while it is pressed: the window buttons, the command bar, the view switch, the offer bar and its X, the row and tile buttons, the "..." menus and the release notes page's buttons. The "Release notes" link underlines.
- The question asked before Hub closes an app to update it is now drawn as a warning, with the warning sign on the bar.
- The release notes page's scrollbar can be dragged by its thumb, a click on its track pages toward the click, and the bar widens under the pointer.
- Settings has a "Window backdrop" choice: Off (the default), Mica or Acrylic, the same backdrop mdedit and mdview use. The title row, the command bar and the status bar let it through while the window has the keyboard; the list and the details stay opaque. It needs Windows 11 22H2 or later with transparency effects on.
